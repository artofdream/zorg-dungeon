the repo as it is on `main`: the TypeScript engine decides outcomes, the
Godot 4.7.2 port presents them, and the golden recordings plus

gdUnit4 stay out. A later Utility AI, or any other AI for heroes or
monsters, has to stay sandboxed and off the win/loss path. Licensed
Humble, Zenva, and Packt packs stay in a private asset store and are not
committed here.

No engine, scene, or ledger row changed. Godot evidence stays
**Simulated**. See [[0008-godot-port-milestone-1]] and [[LEARNINGS-godot]].
