# 2026-11-15

## Godot board lighting

The itch.io web build of level 1.2 was reported as too dark to read: HUD
text was fine, and the checkerboard disappeared into the background. A
local Compatibility render (the web renderer) and a Mobile render (phone
and desktop) of that level matched each other. The lights were the round-9
values, not a renderer that dropped them. Torches worked and were too dim
to carry the floor.

The look is warmer and the empty board glows a little, so tiles, grid
gaps, walls and heroes stay visible without a torch. Damage rooms can
declare a procedural brazier in `apps/godot/levels/visual-lights.json`.
That file is cosmetic. The rules engine and the golden files are unchanged.
Low still uses no shadows and a 0.75 3D scale, with at most 6 omni lights.
High allows 8, the Compatibility per-object cap.

Evidence is a local screenshot pair, not a live itch.io or `/godot/` probe.
Deploys stay unapproved. See [[LEARNINGS-godot]] and [[FINDINGS]] CF-013.
[[0008-godot-port-milestone-1]] still holds: scenes do not decide rules.
