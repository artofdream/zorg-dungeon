# Zorg Dungeon

A small browser-based board / strategy game inspired by Zorg Dungeon from Adam Tsarafidy.

## How to play

Open `/home/runner/work/zorg-dungeon/zorg-dungeon/index.html` in a browser.

- You are the **Delver** (`D`).
- Collect all three **shards** (`S`).
- Reach the **exit** (`E`) after collecting every shard.
- Avoid or defeat **zorglings** (`Z`).
- Walls (`#`) block movement.

Each turn you have **2 actions**. Moving into a zorgling defeats it but still costs an action.  
After your turn ends, every surviving zorgling advances toward you and attacks if adjacent.
